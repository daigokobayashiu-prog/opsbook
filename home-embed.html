The Problem

Your organization already knows how to be ready. That knowledge just isn't connected.

Every organization holds enormous operational knowledge: plans, procedures, playbooks, policies, exercise records, incident reports, after-action reports, and the experience carried by your people. Because it lives in separate systems and separate heads, nobody can see the full picture.

04
Response Depends on Memory

Under pressure, teams fall back on what one or two people remember, not on what the organization has actually learned.

How It Works

Preparedness that keeps working after the exercise ends.

Most resilience programs stop at the after-action report. Opsbook builds a living ontology of your organization, so learning generates outputs at any phase of your cycle, not just after the exercise.

Every cycle makes the next one faster and more accurate, because the organization Opsbook understands keeps getting more complete.

The Platform

Five capabilities. One continuously improving system.

01 · Organizational Intelligence

Understand how your organization works

A continuously improving model of your roles, teams, systems, and dependencies, drawn from the documentation you already have.

02 · Preparedness Intelligence

Know what to fix first

Continuously surfaces missing playbooks, unvalidated procedures, and coverage gaps, and recommends what to prioritize next.

03 · Exercise Intelligence

Design, run, and evaluate exercises

AI-assisted scenario creation, live injects, role-based response workspaces, and after-action reports that produce real evidence.

04 · Operational Learning

Make sure what you learn sticks

Lessons learned and verified improvements stay captured and connected, with evidence and provenance, instead of disappearing after the debrief.

05 · Response Intelligence

Preparedness that extends into response

Opsbook extends preparedness into operational response, assembling role-based guidance from your organization's own validated knowledge.
